# HSC English 1st & 2nd Paper Master Guide

## 1. English 1st Paper (English For Today)
- **Unit 1: People or Personalities Making History**
  * Nelson Mandela: Anti-apartheid leader, reconciliation, from prisoner to president.
  * The Unforgettable History: Historic 7th March Speech of Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman.
  * Two Women: Valentina Tereshkova (first woman in space) & Kalpana Chawla (Columbia space shuttle).
- **Unit 3: Dreams & Ambitions**
  * What is a dream? Philosophical and scientific views (Sigmund Freud).
  * Famous dreams: Martin Luther King Jr. ("I Have a Dream").
- **Unit 4: Traffic Education**
  * Traffic police in Bangladesh, road safety challenges and civic duty.
- **Unit 5: Youth and Adulthood**
  * Challenges of adolescence, emotional and physical changes.
- **Unit 8: Human Rights**
  * Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948), Amerigo (the street child).
- **Unit 12: Environment and Nature**
  * Water pollution, Kuakata (Daughter of the Sea), The Giant Panda conservation.
- **Guided Tasks:** Flowchart completion, Summary writing (within 1/3rd words), Graph/Chart analysis, Theme writing.

## 2. English 2nd Paper (Grammar & Composition)
- **1. Prepositions:** Appropriate prepositions and contextual phrases (e.g., abide by, consist of, adhere to).
- **2. Words / Phrases in Gaps:** *was born, have to / has to, would rather, had better, let alone, what if, as soon as, what's it like*.
- **3. Completing Sentences:** Conditionals, *so that, lest + should, as if / as though, no sooner had... than, hardly had... when, it is high time + past*.
- **4. Right Forms of Verbs:** Subject-verb agreement, active/passive voice, subjunctive mood, tense consistency.
- **5. Narrative Style (Direct to Indirect Speech):** Passage narration transformation.
- **6. Modifiers:**
  * Pre-modifiers: Adjectives, Noun adjectives, Participles (present/past), Quantifiers, Intensifiers (*very, extremely*), Possessives.
  * Post-modifiers: Prepositional phrases, Appositives, Infinitive phrases (*to + V1*), Relative clauses.
- **7. Sentence Connectors:** *Moreover, However, Therefore, On the contrary, In spite of, Consequently, As a result*.
- **8. Synonym & Antonym:** Lexical semantics and contextual vocabulary.
- **9. Punctuation & Capitalization:** Inverted commas, semicolons, colons, hyphens, question marks.
- **Composition (40 Marks):** Formal Letters / Applications, Email writing, Report Writing for Newspaper, Cause & Effect Paragraphs.
