# Class 9-10 English 1st Paper (English For Today) — Master Guide

## 1. Core Textbook Themes and Units
- **Unit 1: Good Citizens**
  * Responsibilities of good citizens: Obeying laws, paying taxes, voting wisely, showing mutual respect.
  * Knowledge, skills, and positive attitude are essential.
- **Unit 3: Events and Festivals**
  * International Mother Language Day (21st February): Commemorating the 1952 Language Martyrs. Recognized globally by UNESCO in 1999.
  * Independence Day (26th March) and Victory Day (16th December).
  * May Day (1st May): Workers' rights and historical 8-hour workday struggle in Chicago in 1886.
  * Pahela Baishakh: Bengali New Year celebrated with Mangal Shobhajatra and traditional festivities.
- **Unit 5: Nature and Humanity**
  * River erosion (Meghna, Jamuna) and displaced populations.
  * Climate Change, Greenhouse Effect, and Global Warming.
  * Deforestation vs. Afforestation and Environmental Conservation.
- **Unit 7: People Who Stand Out**
  * Zainul Abedin: Pioneer of modern Bangladeshi art, renowned for his Famine Sketches of 1943.
  * Pritilata Waddedar: Heroic revolutionary in the anti-British movement.
  * Mother Teresa: Dedicated life to serving the destitute and sick, awarded Nobel Peace Prize.
- **Unit 8: World Heritage Sites in Bangladesh**
  * The Shat Gombuj Mosque in Bagerhat (built by Khan Jahan Ali in the 15th century).
  * The Somapura Mahavihara in Paharpur (ancient Buddhist monastery).
  * The Sundarbans: World's largest mangrove forest, home to the Royal Bengal Tiger.

## 2. Guided Writing Guidelines
- **Paragraph Writing:** Must have a clear Topic Sentence, Supporting Details, and a Concluding Sentence without splitting into multiple paragraphs.
- **Describing Graphs & Charts:**
  * Introduction: State what the graph illustrates.
  * Body: Highlight significant trends, highest/lowest points, and comparative data.
  * Conclusion: Summarize the overall pattern.
- **Story Completion & Informal Letters/Emails:** Logical narrative progression, correct salutation and sign-off.
