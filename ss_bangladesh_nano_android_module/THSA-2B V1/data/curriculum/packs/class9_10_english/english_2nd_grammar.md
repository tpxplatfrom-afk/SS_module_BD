# Class 9-10 English 2nd Paper (English Grammar & Composition) — Master Guide

## 1. Right Forms of Verbs & Subject-Verb Agreement
- **Rule 1 (Singular/Plural):** Singular subject takes singular verb; plural subject takes plural verb.
- **Rule 2 (Universal Truth):** Universal truths and habitual facts always take Simple Present Tense (e.g., The earth moves round the sun).
- **Rule 3 (Modal Auxiliaries):** After can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must, base form (V1) is used.
- **Rule 4 (Conditionals):**
  * 1st Conditional: If + Present Simple, Future Simple (If you study, you will pass).
  * 2nd Conditional: If + Past Simple, Subj + would/could + V1 (If I knew his address, I would write to him).
  * 3rd Conditional: If + Past Perfect, Subj + would have + V3 (If they had played well, they would have won).
- **Rule 5 (Time Adverbials):** Just, just now, already, yet, recently indicate Present Perfect; yesterday, ago, last year indicate Past Simple.

## 2. Transformation of Sentences
- **Voice Change:** Active: Subj + Verb + Obj -> Passive: Obj as Subj + be-verb + V3 + by + Subj as Obj.
- **Degree of Comparison:** Positive (as...as) <-> Comparative (-er / more than) <-> Superlative (the -est / most).
- **Simple, Complex, Compound:**
  * Complex (Since / As / Because / Though / If / When / Relative pronouns): *Though he is poor, he is honest.*
  * Simple (In spite of / Despite / By + V-ing / To + V1): *In spite of his poverty, he is honest.*
  * Compound (and, but, or, yet, so): *He is poor, but he is honest.*
- **Affirmative to Negative:** *Only God can help us.* -> *None but God can help us.* (Nothing but for things, Not more than for numbers).

## 3. Narrative Style (Direct to Indirect Speech)
- Reporting verb: *said to* -> *told* (Assertive), *asked* (Interrogative), *ordered/requested/advised* (Imperative), *prayed/wished* (Optative), *exclaimed with joy/sorrow* (Exclamatory).
- Tense shifting: Present Simple -> Past Simple; Present Continuous -> Past Continuous; Present Perfect -> Past Perfect; Past Simple -> Past Perfect.
- Pronoun and time changes: now -> then, today -> that day, tomorrow -> the next day, yesterday -> the previous day.

## 4. Completing Sentences, Tag Questions & Connectors
- **Connectors:** *Moreover, In addition to, Furthermore, On the other hand, Consequently, Therefore, As a result.*
- **Tag Questions:** Affirmative statement -> Negative tag; Negative statement -> Positive tag.
  * *Everybody loves flowers, don't they?*
  * *Let's go out for a walk, shall we?*
  * *Do not make a noise, will you?*

## 5. Formal Writing: CV with Cover Letter & Applications
- Structure of Cover Letter: Sender's info -> Date -> Receiver's Designation & Address -> Subject -> Salutation -> Body paragraphs -> Complimentary Close.
- Structure of CV: Personal Details, Educational Qualifications (Exam, Year, Board/Univ, GPA), Skills, Experience, References.
